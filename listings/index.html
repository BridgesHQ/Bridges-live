Questions

Good to know

Where do these listings come from?

Active listings come from Stellar MLS through its approved IDX data feed. Each card names the listing brokerage and MLS number. Information is deemed reliable but not guaranteed.

How do I request a showing?

Tap “Request a showing” on any home, live tour or video, or call 813-781-8888. Dorota Maslowska, a licensed broker with LPT Realty, follows up to confirm a private showing in person or on live video.

Can I see every home for sale?

Yes. “Search every MLS home” opens the full MLS search on bridgesglobal.co, with saved searches and new-listing alerts. For Austin, Texas, request a showing and we will send matching homes.
