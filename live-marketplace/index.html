Questions

Good to know

When are tours live?

Agents and builders stream throughout the week. A LIVE badge and real viewer count appear only while a stream is on air; when no one is on air you can ask to be notified the moment the next tour starts.

Can I ask questions during a live tour?

Yes. Join the live chat on any Bridges Live stream to ask about the home, the HOA, schools or the commute, and request a private showing without leaving the stream.

How do agents stream on Bridges Live?

Licensed agents and builders apply on the Stream on Bridges Live page. Once approved they go live from a phone using the Larix app, or paste a YouTube, TikTok or Facebook live link.
