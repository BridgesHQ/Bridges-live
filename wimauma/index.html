Wimauma — new communities on Tampa Bay's growing edge

Wimauma is one of the SouthShore's fastest-growing areas — master-planned new communities, affordable pricing, and room to grow, minutes from the bay.

By Dorota Maslowska, Broker Associate · Bridges Global · FL BK3519799

Why Wimauma is growing fast

Wimauma has become a new-construction hotspot on the SouthShore — master-planned communities like Southshore Bay (with its Lagoon) offer resort living at accessible prices. It's where builders and value-focused buyers are moving.

New construction & the Lagoon

Communities here feature amenities you rarely find at this price — including a crystal lagoon. We tour models and quick-move-in homes live and flag the best builder incentives.

Value & growth

Some of the most affordable new homes in the metro, with strong appreciation potential as the SouthShore continues to expand.

Is Wimauma right for you?

If you want a new home with resort amenities at an accessible price, Wimauma deserves a look. We'll tour communities with you on camera.
