Go Live

Stream from your phone with your own stream key, or paste a YouTube / TikTok / Facebook live link. Either way your live appears first on the homepage and the Bridges Live grid, with chat and the Request-a-showing button.

How to go live in 5 steps

  1. Get the free Larix Broadcaster appiPhone: App Store · Android: Google Play. (On a computer, OBS Studio works the same way.)
  2. Tap "Start My Live" aboveChoose "Stream from my phone", type what you're showing, and tap Start. Your stream URL and key appear here — they stay the same every time.
  3. Add the connection in Larix (one time only)Larix → ⚙️ Settings → Connections → + → paste the Full Larix URL (tap "Copy full Larix URL") → Save. In OBS: Settings → Stream → Custom → paste Server and Stream key.
  4. Hold the phone vertical and press the red buttonWithin about 20 seconds your card flips to LIVE on the homepage and the grid. Viewers chat, and "Request a showing" sends leads to you.
  5. Finish: stop in Larix, then tap "End live" hereLeads from the stream are already in 📥 Leads and in your email. No phone key yet? Go live on YouTube or TikTok and paste the link instead.

How to Operate

Every job on Bridges Live, in plain steps — with a button that takes you straight there. Bookmark this page on your phone.

1 · Log in

  1. Go to live.bridgesglobal.co/admin (bookmark it — there is no admin link on the public site).
  2. Password: your ADMIN_TOKEN. Find it in Render → bridges-live → Environment → ADMIN_TOKEN (click the eye icon).
  3. Leave "Keep me logged in on this device" ticked on your own phone. Use Log out (top right) on shared computers.

2 · Go live

  1. 🔴 Go Live → type what you're showing → choose Stream from my phone (or Paste a live link for YouTube / TikTok / Facebook) → Start My Live.
  2. Phone: open Larix Broadcaster and press the red button (first time: follow the 5 steps on the Go Live tab).
  3. Within ~20 seconds you're first on the homepage and the grid with a red LIVE badge and the real number of viewers.
  4. Finish: stop in Larix, then End live on the Go Live tab.

3 · Where the videos and streams are

  • On the site: the big player at the top of the homepage, the "Live tours & recent videos" row under it, and the full grid at /live-marketplace.
  • Each tile is either a real live stream (red LIVE badge), a recent video from your YouTube / Instagram / TikTok, or an Upcoming tile. Nothing is ever shown as live unless it really is.
  • 🎥 Videos & recordings shows every video pulled from your accounts. Tap Hide on anything that isn't a property video. Sync now pulls the newest ones immediately (it also runs automatically every 15 minutes).
  • To add one video by hand, paste its YouTube / TikTok / Instagram link in Add a video by link.

4 · Download a recorded live stream

  1. Every phone live is recorded automatically by Cloudflare.
  2. 🎥 Videos & recordings → Recordings → find the date → Prepare MP4.
  3. Wait a minute or two, tap Refresh, then Download MP4 — it saves to your phone or computer, ready for Instagram, TikTok or YouTube.
  4. Tap Show on site to add the recording to the video grid as a replay.

5 · Where leads show up

  • 📥 Leads — everyone who requested a showing, filled a form, chatted or tapped WhatsApp. New ones have a gold edge and count on the 🏠 Today tab.
  • Email — every new lead is emailed to you instantly with Call / WhatsApp / Email buttons.
  • HubSpot — every lead is added to your CRM automatically.

6 · Respond to a lead

  1. In 📥 Leads tap 📞 Call, 💬 Text, WhatsApp or ✉️ Email — a friendly first message is already written for you.
  2. The lead is marked Contacted automatically. Change the status as things move: Showing booked → Under contract → Closed (or Lost).
  3. Each lead also gets 5 automatic follow-up emails over 14 days. Stop them in 📣 Follow-ups once you're talking directly.

7 · Listings on the site

  • 🏡 Listings shows where homes for sale come from (Stellar MLS when the token is connected) and when they last updated. They refresh every hour; Sync now refreshes immediately.
  • Only real listings are shown, each with its listing brokerage. If no feed is connected, the site shows a button to your full MLS search on bridgesglobal.co.

8 · Post today's video

  1. 🎬 Today's Content → read the hook and script → film each shot in the shotlist.
  2. Tap Copy caption + hashtags, fill in any [bracketed] numbers, post, then set the status to Posted ✓.

9 · Other agents

  • Agents apply at /become-a-streamer. Check their license, then Approve in 👥 Agent streams — they get a private Go-Live link by email. Take down removes a stream immediately.

10 · Something not working?

  • ⚙️ Setup lists every connection (database, email, CRM, streaming, social accounts, listings) with exactly what to add in Render if one is missing.
