Apollo Beach, Florida — waterfront living & new homes

On Tampa Bay's South Shore, Apollo Beach pairs genuine waterfront living with strong master-planned new construction like Waterset and Del Webb — a rare combination south of Tampa.

By Dorota Maslowska, Broker Associate · Bridges Global

Apollo Beach is one of the most distinctive communities in the Tampa Bay region: an actual waterfront town on the South Shore, with canal-front homes, marinas, and Gulf access, alongside some of the area's most popular master-planned new construction. For buyers who want water and a new home without Sarasota or beachfront pricing, Apollo Beach is a standout.

Why buyers choose Apollo Beach

The draw is lifestyle: waterfront access, boating, and a relaxed South Shore feel, combined with newer communities and amenities. It has grown quickly as buyers discovered you can have Gulf-connected living at attainable prices within a reasonable commute of Tampa and MacDill.

New construction & communities

Apollo Beach is home to Waterset, one of the region's premier master-planned communities, and Del Webb Southshore, a sought-after 55+ resort community. Both offer new construction with builder incentives. As always, bring your own broker before your first visit to protect those incentives — and compare communities live on Bridges Live before you drive out.

Waterfront comes with homework. Flood zones, insurance, and canal vs. open-water access all affect cost and value. We walk you through it before you commit.

Getting around

Apollo Beach sits just off US-41 and I-75 on the South Shore, convenient to Brandon, MacDill Air Force Base, and downtown Tampa, with Sarasota and the beaches an easy drive south.

Is Apollo Beach right for you?

  • You want waterfront or water-community living
  • You want new construction with resort-style amenities (Waterset)
  • You are 55+ and want an active-adult community (Del Webb)
  • You want Gulf-connected living at attainable prices
