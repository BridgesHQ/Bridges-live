How apartment locating works

1. Tell us what you need

Budget, move-in date, number of bedrooms, must-haves like pet-friendly, parking, or a specific school zone. In English or Polish.

2. We match and tour it live

We pull communities that fit and walk them for you on live video — so you see the real unit, the real finishes, the real view, and ask questions in the moment.

3. Claim it and apply

When you find the one, claim it on Bridges Live and we coordinate the application, any move-in specials, and the timeline.
