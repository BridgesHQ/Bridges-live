Miami — global luxury & waterfront living

Miami is a global city where international buyers, luxury waterfront, and a no-income-tax lifestyle meet. We help buyers worldwide navigate it — live, on camera, in your language.

By Dorota Maslowska, Broker Associate · Bridges Global · FL BK3519799

Why Miami

Miami is a truly international market — a magnet for buyers from Latin America, Europe, and across the U.S., drawn by waterfront luxury, finance and tech growth, and no state income tax.

Luxury condos & waterfront

From Brickell high-rises to Coral Gables and the beaches, Miami's luxury market is vast. We tour condos and waterfront homes live so international buyers see the real unit before traveling.

For international buyers

Miami is built on global capital. We work with international and relocating buyers in English & Polish, coordinating tours in your time zone and guiding the whole process.

Is Miami right for you?

Waterfront, luxury, or investment — share your goals and budget and we'll match properties and stream them live, wherever you are in the world.
