Who I am

I'm Dorota Maslowska, a Broker Associate serving Tampa Bay and Austin. I built my practice around one idea: buying or renting a home shouldn't depend on where you happen to live or what language you speak. Whether you're moving across town, across the country, or across an ocean, you deserve to see the real home, ask real questions, and work with someone who has your interests in mind — not the builder's, not the landlord's.

One side is centuries of practice. The other side is now. I work in the space in between.

What makes my practice different

I'm licensed in both Florida and Texas — two of the largest migration markets in the country — and I work fully in English & Polish. That combination lets me help relocation buyers and international clients who are too often shown a home only in a language and a market they don't yet know.

I also founded Bridges Live, a live home-tour platform built on a simple truth from live shopping everywhere else: people buy what they can see, in real time, with their questions answered on the spot. Builders, apartment communities, and agents tour homes on camera, and buyers claim what they love live.

How I can help you

New construction with your incentives protected. Apartment locating. Relocation guidance that starts before you land. Selling your home to buyers local marketing never reaches. Whatever the move, I guide the whole thing.
