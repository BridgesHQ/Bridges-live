Questions

Good to know

What is Bridges Live?

Bridges Live is a live video platform for Tampa Bay real estate. Licensed agents and builders stream homes, model homes and neighborhoods; viewers chat, ask questions and request a private showing in one tap. Bridges Global is a marketing and technology service, not a brokerage.

How do I request a showing?

Tap “Request a showing” on any home, live tour or video, or call 813-781-8888. Dorota Maslowska, a licensed broker with LPT Realty, follows up to confirm a private showing in person or on live video. There is no fee to buyers or renters.

Who is the broker behind Bridges Global?

Dorota Maslowska, Broker Associate with LPT Realty, licensed in Florida (BK3519799) and Texas (829789-SA). She works in English and Polish with buyers, sellers and renters relocating to Tampa Bay.

Does it cost anything to use?

No. There are no fees to buyers or renters. Compensation is paid by property owners and builders as allowed by state law, and earnest money is never collected through the website.
