Questions

Good to know

What is Bridges Live?

Bridges Live is a live video platform for real estate. Licensed agents and builders stream homes, model homes, apartments and neighborhoods; viewers chat, ask questions and request a private showing in one tap. Bridges Global is a marketing and technology service, not a brokerage.

How do I request a showing?

Tap “Request a showing” on any home, live tour or video, or call 813-781-8888. Dorota Maslowska, a licensed broker with LPT Realty, follows up to confirm a private showing in person or on live video.

Which markets do you cover?

Tampa Bay and Sarasota in Florida, and Austin, Texas. Relocation clients moving from other states and other countries are toured live on video in their own time zone.

Who is the broker behind Bridges Global?

Dorota Maslowska, Broker Associate with LPT Realty, licensed in Florida (BK3519799) and Texas (829789-SA). She works in English and Polish with buyers, sellers and renters, including clients relocating from other states and countries.
