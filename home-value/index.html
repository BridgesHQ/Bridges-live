In short

A home valuation from Bridges Global is a licensed broker's opinion of what your home will sell for, not an automated estimate. Dorota Maslowska, Broker Associate with LPT Realty, studies recent comparable sales and active competition in Tampa Bay, Sarasota or Austin, adjusts for condition and upgrades, and explains the range she recommends.

Questions

Good to know

How is a broker's valuation different from an online estimate?

Online estimates use public records and cannot see your home's condition, upgrades or the street it sits on. A broker compares recent nearby sales in person and explains the reasoning behind the range.

Do I have to list my home after a valuation?

No. A valuation carries no obligation to list or sell.
