Builder incentives

Know what's on the table before you sign.

Builders change incentives often, and they differ by community, floor plan and lender. Ask on the live tour and we confirm the current ones in writing with the builder before you commit.

Request a showing
01

Closing-cost credits

Builders often contribute toward closing costs, usually tied to using their preferred lender.

02

Rate buydowns

A builder may buy down your mortgage rate for the first years, or for the life of the loan.

03

Design-studio upgrades

Upgrade allowances for flooring, cabinets, appliances and finishes chosen at the design center.

04

Quick move-in homes

Finished or nearly finished homes the builder wants sold now, often with their own pricing.

Incentives are offered by builders, vary by community and change without notice. Nothing here is an offer or a guarantee.

Do I need my own agent to buy from a builder?

You don't have to, but your own broker represents you rather than the builder. Register with your broker before you first tour the sales center.
